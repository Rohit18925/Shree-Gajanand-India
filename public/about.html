Who We Are

Your Trusted Partner In Government Contracting

Shree Gajanand India is a service and contract-based company working with government departments, public sector organisations and infrastructure projects. We undertake work according to project and tender requirements, including manpower supply, housekeeping, facility management, sanitation, maintenance, operational support and other related services. Our approach is simple — understand the requirement, arrange the right manpower and resources, supervise the work regularly and make sure the assigned job is carried out properly. We are not limited to one department or one sector. Our objective is to work across different government and public-sector projects wherever our experience, resources and eligibility meet the project requirement.

Our Mission

Our aim is to become a dependable contracting partner for our clients by providing trained manpower, proper supervision and timely execution of work. We focus on doing the assigned work correctly and maintaining a professional relationship with every client.

Our Commitment

Once a project starts, we stay involved in its day-to-day execution. We pay attention to manpower availability, cleanliness, safety, work quality and communication with the client so that issues can be handled quickly and the work continues smoothly.

Why Choose Us

On-Site Supervision

We provide manpower according to the requirement of the project and work to maintain regular attendance and discipline.

Timely Execution

We understand that government and infrastructure contracts run on schedules, so completing work on time remains an important priority.

Safety & Work Discipline

Our teams are expected to follow site instructions, basic safety requirements and the working standards of the client.

Direct Communication

We believe problems are easier to solve when communication is clear. We stay available to coordinate with the client throughout the project.

“Our work is judged at the site, not by what we write in a brochure. That is why we focus on regular supervision, responsible manpower and completing the work properly.”

— Management Team, Shree Gajanand India
