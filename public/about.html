Who We Are

Your Trusted Partner for Multi-Sector Service Solutions

Shree Gajanand India Pvt. Ltd. is a growing service and contract-based company built around a simple idea: good work begins with dependable people, clear communication and a genuine commitment to every client. We work across cleaning and sanitation, housekeeping and facility management, security, construction, railway-related works, manpower support, manufacturing and other allied services. Rather than trying to be everything to everyone, we focus on understanding the actual requirement at each site and delivering practical, disciplined and quality-conscious solutions. Our aim is to build long-term relationships through honest communication, responsible execution and consistent service. We believe that when a company understands its clients, respects its workforce and takes responsibility for the work it undertakes, good results follow naturally.
