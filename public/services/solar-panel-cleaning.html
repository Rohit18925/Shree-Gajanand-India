Cleaning of Solar Panels

Home / Services / Cleaning of Solar Panels

Housekeeping & Facility Care

Dust and bird droppings on panels reduce how much power they produce. We clean on a regular cycle, taking care not to scratch the glass or disturb the wiring.

What this includes

  • Dry and wet cleaning of panels
  • Rooftop and ground-mounted arrays
  • Cleaning schedule set by dust levels
  • Safe movement and access on roofs
  • Soft brushes and clean water, no harsh chemicals

Where we provide it

  • Rooftop plants
  • Ground-mounted plants
  • Industrial sites
  • Institutions
