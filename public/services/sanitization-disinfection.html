Housekeeping & Facility Care

Places with heavy footfall need more than ordinary cleaning. We sanitise and disinfect on a routine, with extra rounds for high-touch spots such as handrails, door handles, counters and switches.

  • Washroom and toilet disinfection
  • High-touch surface sanitising
  • Sanitising of waiting and common areas
  • Supply and refilling of hygiene consumables
  • Routine rounds fixed with the site in-charge

Where we provide it

  • Stations
  • Offices
  • Cafeterias
  • Healthcare-related sites
