Housekeeping & Facility Care

Equipment alone does not keep a site clean; habits do. We work with staff and the site team on simple practices such as segregating waste, saving water and power, and keeping common areas tidy.

What this includes

  • Awareness talks for site staff
  • Waste segregation practice
  • Water and energy saving habits
  • Display of simple do and don't guidance
  • Follow-up visits to check that habits continue

Where we provide it

  • Offices
  • Stations
  • Campuses
  • Industrial sites
